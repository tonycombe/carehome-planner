📅 Availability & Ride Rota

Please keep your availability up to date using the Doodle rota. Keeping your availability current helps the Chapter Captain plan rides and ensures our care homes receive a reliable service.

🗓 Open Doodle Availability

🚲 Before Every Ride

  • ✔ Carry out the pre-ride safety inspection.
  • ✔ Check battery charge and brakes.
  • ✔ Ensure passengers are comfortable and secure.
  • ✔ Confirm today's ride schedule.
  • ✔ Carry emergency contact details.

🤝 Volunteer Commitments

  • Perform your volunteering role to the best of your ability.
  • Follow all CWAS procedures, guidance and training.
  • Protect confidential information.
  • Meet agreed commitments and give notice if unavailable.
  • Complete required training and PVG checks.

❤️ CWAS Commitments

  • Provide training and ongoing support.
  • Provide insurance while volunteering.
  • Treat all volunteers fairly and equally.
  • Help resolve any issues quickly.
  • Respect your availability and volunteering time.
